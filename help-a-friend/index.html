What to do right now

  1. Call 911.Say where you are, that someone drank too much, and what you see.
  2. Don't leave them alone.Stay with them until help arrives, even if they seem to be sleeping.
  3. Turn them on their side.Top knee bent, head tilted a little back. This keeps them from choking if they throw up.
  4. Keep them warm.Cover them with a jacket or blanket.
  5. Tell the responders what you know.What they drank, how much, and when. Mention any pills or other drugs.

These don't work

"Let them sleep it off." Alcohol keeps entering the blood after they stop drinking. They can get worse while asleep.
Coffee or a cold shower. Neither sobers anyone up. A cold shower can make them colder and more at risk.
Making them throw up. They could choke. Keep them on their side instead.

You won't get in trouble for helping

Colorado law protects people who call 911 for someone having an alcohol or drug emergency, as long as you stay with them and cooperate with responders. The person who needs help is protected too.

Saving your friend matters more than any rule you broke tonight.
