Save the app to your phone or computer

This puts a coyote icon on your home screen, desktop or dock. It opens like an app, with no app store and nothing to download.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari. It will not work from inside an email or another app, so use Safari's address bar if needed.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The coyote icon appears on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if you see it.
  4. Tap Install or Add, then confirm. The coyote icon appears on your home screen.

Computer

  1. Chrome: click the three dots at the top right, then Cast, save and share, then Install page as app.
  2. Edge: click the three dots, then Apps, then Install this site as an app.
  3. Safari on a Mac: choose File, then Add to Dock.

Already saved it before and still see a plain icon? Delete the old one and add it again.
