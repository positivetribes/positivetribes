01 / How we work

See it before
you pay for it.

The usual way of getting software built was designed for big organizations with big budgets. We start with a conversation and put something you can click through in front of you within days.

The old way

  1. Write an RFP
  2. Months of discovery
  3. A 400-point requirements document
  4. Your first look comes after you’ve already paid

The Positive Tribes way

  1. 01

    A conversation

    Tell us about the people you serve and what’s getting in the way. You don’t need a polished brief.

  2. 02

    A mock-up in days

    A clickable mock-up your team can try, so we agree on what’s needed before anything is built.

  3. 03

    A phased build

    We build in small phases you can test along the way, then host and support it after launch.

02 / What we’re building

Real nonprofits.
Real problems.

What we’re working on now with nonprofits around the country. Each one started with a conversation and a mock-up. Some names are left out until our partners are ready to share them.

↗

SCHEDULING · 84 GYMS

A national Parkinson’s climbing nonprofit

It was paying $14K a year for gym software that couldn’t report across its 84 locations. We proposed one scheduling and registration tool with reporting across every gym, hosted and supported for less.

WILDLIFE · NATIONWIDE

Project Coyote

Tracking coyotes with mange across the country: people report sightings, the team reviews them, and alerts go out so help can reach them.
