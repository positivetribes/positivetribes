Volunteer

Lend your skills to healthier communities.

Positive Tribes is a Golden, Colorado nonprofit building simple apps and digital tools with other nonprofits. We are looking for people who want to help, whether or not you work in technology.

Ways to help

Build

Developers and designers who want to turn a nonprofit's practical idea into a simple, usable tool.

Test and advise

Try our early apps and tell us honestly what works. Nonprofit staff and health or wellness professionals are especially welcome as advisors.

Connect

Know a nonprofit with a challenge a small tool could solve? An introduction is a valuable way to help.

Tell us about you

Send us a few lines about how you would like to help. Your details are used only to reply to you; see our privacy policy.
