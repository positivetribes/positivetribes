Care plans

Your own software team, without the payroll.

Most small nonprofits have no tech staff. A volunteer or the director keeps things running on the side. A care plan gives you what a tech team would: the tool we built for you stays up, stays safe and keeps getting better every month.

A director asks for a change on Monday. On Thursday they’re testing it, and by Friday the whole team is using it.

You ask for improvements in your private hub. We tell you the size of each one before any work starts, you test it on a safe copy with sample data, and nothing goes live until you sign off.

Three plans, one simple price each

Every plan covers hosting, backups, monitoring and fixes to what we built. Fixes are always free, and so are small wording, label and image changes. Credits pay for improvements, and unused credits roll over for a month.

A software team, not an IT department

Included

  • The tools we built, with their hosting, backups and monitoring
  • Their connections to your CRM, texting and email
  • The data inside those tools, kept private to your organization
  • Fixes, small edits and monthly improvements

Not included

  • Laptops, phones, printers and Wi-Fi
  • Email accounts, passwords and Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace admin

Something bigger, like a new screen or a new connection? We’ll explain why and quote it as its own project, so there are no surprise bills.
