What we’re building

Real nonprofits. Real problems.

What we’re working on now with nonprofits around the country. Each one started with a conversation and a clickable mock-up. Some names are left out until our partners are ready to share them.

Scheduling · 84 gyms

A national Parkinson’s climbing nonprofit

Scheduling and registration for climbers across 84 partner gyms. The organization was paying $14K a year for gym software that couldn’t report across its locations. We proposed one tool with reporting across every gym, hosted and supported for less.

Wildlife · Nationwide

Project Coyote

Tracking coyotes with mange across the country: people report sightings, the team reviews them, and alerts go out so help can reach them.

Recovery · Chapters

Sober AF Entertainment

Chapter expansion and host training for sober social events, from applying to host through certification and event approval.

Ideas we’d like to explore

What could a focused tool do for your community? These are possible projects, not completed client work.

  • Volunteer coordination: find opportunities, organize shifts and keep volunteers connected to your mission.
  • Connection for older adults: help people discover local activities and stay involved in their community.
  • Mental-health resources: help people find trusted resources and the support your organization offers.
  • Wellness challenges: encourage movement, mindfulness and healthy habits through shared activities.
  • Client check-ins: simple ways to stay in touch, gather feedback and keep people engaged with a program.

From our lab

PulseLift

A health and fitness app bringing workouts, training plans, blood-pressure logs and medicine tracking into one place. Now available to the public.

Want to help test it? Reach out and we will get you set up.

Volunteer Connect

A prototype of a simpler front door for volunteer records kept in Salesforce. It is not publicly hosted.

How we work

  1. A conversation. Tell us about the people you serve and what’s getting in the way.
  2. A mock-up in days. A clickable mock-up your team can try, so we agree on what’s needed before anything is built.
  3. A phased build. We build in small phases you can test along the way, then host and support it after launch.
