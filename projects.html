Ideas in development

Small tools. Meaningful possibilities.

We are exploring how thoughtful technology can help nonprofit teams and communities. The examples below are prototypes and demonstrations, not completed client engagements.

PulseLift

A working app example bringing workouts, training frequency, blood-pressure logs and medicine tracking into one place.

Now available to the public. Want to help test it? Reach out and we will get you set up.

Health and wellness tools

Simple interfaces to help people organize personal routines and records. We are exploring how tools like these might support nonprofit programs.

Volunteer Connect

A Salesforce integration prototype for viewing contacts and adding volunteers through a focused interface. The prototype is not publicly hosted.

How we work

Listen to an organization's needs, identify a small practical problem, build an early version, test it with real users and improve together. We welcome conversations about potential future collaborations.
