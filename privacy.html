Privacy

Your privacy matters to us.

This page explains what information the Positive Tribes website collects, how we use it and the choices you have. Last updated October 5, 2026.

What we collect

Messages you send us. When you use our contact, volunteer or support form, we receive your name, email address and message, and any screenshot you choose to attach to a support request. We use them only to reply to you and to follow up on your request.

Website usage. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, and to see whether the contact and volunteer forms are being used. Google Analytics collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and uses cookies or similar technologies to do so. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Server logs. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes requests to the site and may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not ask for health, medical or financial details through this website, and we ask that you do not send sensitive client or health information in a message.

Donations

Gifts are processed by Stripe on its secure checkout page; Positive Tribes never sees or stores your card or bank details. We receive your name, email address, billing address and gift amount, and keep them in our donor records (Salesforce) to record your gift, send receipts and thank you. We do not sell or share donor information.

Who can see your information

Messages and support requests go to Positive Tribes by email and are saved in our contact database (Salesforce) so we can follow up. Screenshots you attach to a support request are stored with that request. Information is shared with the service providers that run the site, its analytics and that database (Cloudflare, Google, Salesforce), and when the law requires it. We use your details only for Positive Tribes work, and you can ask us to correct or delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email hello@positivetribes.org.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
