Our organization

Technology in service of people.

Positive Tribes is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Golden, Colorado. Our mission is to strengthen health, wellness and community connection through accessible technology and collaboration with organizations that serve others.

Our mission and approach

We explore and develop practical digital tools that can help nonprofits connect with their communities, simplify everyday work and support healthy lives. We begin by listening to organizations and the people they serve, then test focused ideas and improve them through feedback.

Our current work includes PulseLift, a health and fitness app example, and Volunteer Connect, a prototype exploring a simpler interface for nonprofit volunteer records using Salesforce. These examples illustrate our approach; they are not a claim of completed client programs or partnerships.
