Our organization

Technology in service of people.

Positive Tribes is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Golden, Colorado. Our mission is to strengthen health, wellness and community connection through accessible technology and collaboration with organizations that serve others.

Our mission and approach

We build practical digital tools that help nonprofits connect with their communities, simplify everyday work and support healthy lives. We work AI-native, so we move as fast as the newest AI-first companies: we start with a conversation, show you a clickable mock-up within days, then build in phases and host and support what we build.

We work with small to mid-size nonprofits anywhere in the US, especially those without IT staff. Our current work includes scheduling and registration for a national Parkinson’s climbing nonprofit, tracking coyotes with mange for Project Coyote, and chapter expansion and host training for Sober AF Entertainment.

Meet our founder

Eric Roberts · Founder & CEO

After three decades of building technology for businesses, Eric started Positive Tribes to put that experience to work where it's needed most. He saw that the organizations doing the most good often have the least access to good tools. He works alongside nonprofits and community groups to build technology that fits how they actually work: simple enough to use on a busy day, and useful enough to give people time back for the work that matters.

Eric has been building technology since 1995, turning complex systems into practical tools. He holds Salesforce administrator and app builder certifications and is a certified project manager and ScrumMaster, so partners get someone who listens first, builds in small steps and improves through real feedback. That approach is behind the tools Positive Tribes is building today.

Based in Golden, Colorado, Eric is guided by a phrase he lives by: finding the extraordinary in the ordinary. To him, healthier communities are built the same way, through everyday tools, everyday people and small connections that add up.

Our advisors

Rich Sonnenblick · Advisor

Rich founded Enrich, decision-analytics software that helps organizations choose which R&D and capital projects to fund. Planview acquired it in 2022, and it lives on today as Planview Advisor. As Planview’s Chief Data Scientist, he grew a global data science team that delivered AI features, and he speaks internationally on AI in drug development. He holds a PhD from Carnegie Mellon. He helps shape how Positive Tribes uses AI to build faster for nonprofits.

Glenn Hadley · Advisor

Glenn is co-founder and Chief Growth Officer of JGHealthcare Solutions and a partner in Apt Financial, a payments platform built for behavioral healthcare. He also serves on the board of Sober AF Entertainment. He brings marketing and behavioral health experience to Positive Tribes’ work with recovery and health nonprofits.
