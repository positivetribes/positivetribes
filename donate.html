Turn code into capacity

Positive Tribes builds technology for small, grassroots nonprofits, the organizations doing vital work with almost no budget. Your gift turns code into capacity they could never otherwise afford.

We focus on small organizations on purpose. They are the ones who genuinely cannot hire a developer, so the tools we build are the difference between having what they need and going without. Every donation funds the impact, and the development work is simply how we deliver it.
