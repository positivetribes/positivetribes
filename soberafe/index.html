Lived experience is the credential.

The 2026 peer support specialist cohort is fully funded: 150 COPA-approved seats, free for residents 18 and older in Adams, Denver, Boulder, Weld and Mesa counties. Peers meet people where they are, at the venue, on the campus quad, in the green room before a show.

Sober AF

Sober AF Entertainment (SAFE) is a 501(c)(3) recovery community organization. We believe connection to community is a key part of long-term recovery, and we create sober sections at music festivals, concerts and sporting events.
