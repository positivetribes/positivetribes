Up ENDing Parkinson's

Privacy Policy

Up ENDing Parkinson's, registered for text messaging as Up ENDing Parkinsons ("UEP"), is a nonprofit that runs free and low-cost rock climbing programs for people with Parkinson's disease and their care partners. This notice explains how we handle your mobile number and the text messages we send you for the UEP Climb Scheduler.

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, and whether you agreed to receive texts.
  • The date and time you agreed, and the page where you agreed.
  • A record of the texts we sent to your number.

How we use it

  • To send you a sign-in code when you ask to sign in.
  • To send you a reminder 3 days before each climb you have booked, if you ticked the box to receive reminders.

We do not send marketing or promotional texts from this number.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text message originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share your number only with the service providers that deliver the messages and run the scheduler for UEP: Twilio (text delivery), Cloudflare (hosting), and Positive Tribes (which builds and maintains the scheduler for UEP). They use your information only to provide their service. UEP does not sell your information.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts. We will send one message to confirm.
  • Reply START to begin receiving texts again.
  • Untick the text box on your Profile page in the scheduler.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email upendingparkinsons@gmail.com.

Message and data rates may apply. You do not need to agree to texts to take part in the program; you can use email instead.

How long we keep it

We keep your number while you take part in the program. You can ask us to update or delete it at any time by emailing upendingparkinsons@gmail.com.
