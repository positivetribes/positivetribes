These terms apply to text messages from Up ENDing Parkinson's, registered for text messaging as Up ENDing Parkinsons ("UEP") through the UEP Climb Scheduler.

SMS Terms: the program

If you agree, UEP sends you (1) a sign-in code when you ask to sign in, and (2) a reminder 3 days before each climb you have booked. Example: "Up ENDing Parkinson's: you're booked to climb at Sportrock Alexandria on Sat, Nov 14 at 10:00am. Reply STOP to stop."

How you agree

You agree by ticking the box on the sign-up or Profile page that reads: "Text me reminders 3 days before each climb I book, and sign-in codes. From Up ENDing Parkinson's. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help." You can also ask for a sign-in code by text on the sign-in page. Agreeing to texts is not required to take part in the program.

How often

Message frequency varies. You get at most one reminder per climb you book, plus a sign-in code each time you ask for one.

Cost

UEP does not charge for texts. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

Stop and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop texts. You will get one message confirming that you are unsubscribed.
  • Reply START to start again.
  • Reply HELP, or email upendingparkinsons@gmail.com, for help.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Messages are sent to US mobile numbers only.

Your information

How we handle your mobile number is explained in our Privacy Policy. No mobile information is shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
