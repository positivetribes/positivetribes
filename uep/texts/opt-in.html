Text Message Sign-Up

Up ENDing Parkinson's, registered for text messaging as Up ENDing Parkinsons ("UEP"), runs free and low-cost climbing programs for people with Parkinson's disease and their care partners. Climbers sign up for UEP climbs in the UEP Climb Scheduler. This page shows how a person agrees to text messages from UEP while signing up.

This page shows the sign-up step so that you can see the consent wording. It is a sample form and does not collect or send any information. The live sign-up form is in the UEP Climb Scheduler, launching in early 2027.

The consent checkbox

On the sign-up page, and again on the Profile page, a person sees this. The checkbox is never ticked for them. Agreeing to texts is optional and is not required to sign up or to climb with UEP.

By signing up you also agree to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Another way to agree

A person can also ask for a one-time sign-in code by text on the sign-in page. Entering their mobile number and requesting the code means they agree to receive that code by text.

What we send

  • One-time sign-in codes, only when the person asks for one.
  • One reminder 3 days before each climb the person has booked.

No marketing or promotional texts. Reply STOP at any time to stop, START to start again, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
